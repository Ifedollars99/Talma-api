Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. About Talma

Talma is a social learning platform for students and lecturers. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and share it, and the choices available to you. In this policy, “Talma”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the Talma service.

If you have questions about this policy or your personal data, contact us at taofeeqifedollar@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: your username, email address, password (stored only as an Argon2id hash), account role, and optional profile details such as avatar, bio, gender, country, state, language and timezone.
  • Learning and community content: posts, messages, community memberships, course enrolments, assignment submissions, practice-test attempts and files you upload.
  • Creator information: display name, bio, bank name, account number, account name, Paystack subaccount code and creator application or payout status.
  • Payment information: transaction references, amounts, currency, payment status and creator/platform revenue splits. Card details are collected and processed by Paystack; Talma does not store full card numbers.
  • YouTube and video information: when an approved creator uploads a lesson, the video file is sent directly from the creator’s browser to YouTube using the Talma channel’s authorisation. Talma stores the YouTube video ID, title, description, duration, privacy status and related upload metadata. Talma never receives your Google password.
  • Usage and technical information: IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, timestamps, error logs and security events.
  • Cookies and local storage: a refresh-token cookie used to keep you signed in, your theme preference, and LINT conversation history stored in your browser’s local storage.

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage accounts, authenticate users and restore sessions.
  • To provide the feed, courses, messages, communities, tests and other features.
  • To process payments, calculate creator and platform shares, and pay creators.
  • To upload, review, publish and deliver creator lessons.
  • To send transactional email such as verification, password-reset and creator emails.
  • To keep the platform safe, prevent abuse, investigate reports and enforce our terms.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot and improve the service, and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Google and YouTube API Services

Talma uses YouTube API Services to upload creator lessons to the Talma YouTube channel and to read video metadata. By using Talma’s video features, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and acknowledge the Google Privacy Policy.

Talma’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not use YouTube data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

You can revoke Talma’s access to your Google account at any time through your Google security settings. Revoking access may prevent creator uploads and other video features from working. Note that creator uploads are sent to the Talma YouTube channel; the Google account that authorises the channel is the channel owner’s account, not necessarily the creator’s personal Google account.

5. How we share information

We share personal data only with service providers and in the limited cases below:

  • Paystack for payment processing, transaction verification and creator payouts.
  • Google and YouTube when a creator uploads a lesson or when video metadata is read or updated.
  • Email providers (such as Resend or an SMTP provider) to deliver transactional email.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that store data and run the service on our behalf.
  • Legal and safety disclosures when required by law or necessary to protect Talma, our users or the public.
  • Business transfers if Talma is merged, acquired or its assets are transferred, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell your personal data.

6. Data retention

We keep account and content data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise the associated personal data, except where we must keep records for legal, tax, fraud-prevention or payment-dispute purposes. Purchase and payout records may be retained for as long as required by applicable law. Server logs are kept for a limited period and then deleted or aggregated.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed with Argon2id. Access tokens are short-lived, refresh tokens are stored in HttpOnly cookies, and production traffic is served over HTTPS. Access to personal data is restricted to authorised personnel and systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to request a portable copy. You can update most profile information in your account settings and delete your account from the settings screen. To make another request, contact taofeeqifedollar@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Children

Talma is intended for students and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires a higher age). If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child below the applicable age without appropriate consent, we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Talma and its service providers may process data in countries other than the one where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated through the service where appropriate.

12. Contact us

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email taofeeqifedollar@gmail.com. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.
